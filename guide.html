FLASHING GUIDE

Automated Fastboot installation guide for Redmi K50i / Poco X4 GT (xaga). Choose your flashing environment below.

⚠️

Crucial Prerequisites Before Flashing

1. Unlocked Bootloader: Ensure your Redmi K50i bootloader is unlocked via official Mi Unlock tool.
2. Data Backup: A clean flash formats internal storage. Back up all photos, documents, and 2FA credentials.
3. Install Drivers: Install proper Xiaomi Fastboot & ADB USB drivers on your PC so your device is recognized without issues.
4. Battery Level: Ensure your phone has at least 50% battery to prevent interruption.
5. Enter Fastboot Mode: Turn off device, hold Volume Down + Power until the orange Fastboot logo appears.

🔥 Clean Flash (Recommended)
Mandatory when coming from MIUI/HyperOS, another AOSP ROM, or across major Android versions. Formats userdata cleanly.
⚡ Dirty Flash / Update
Only supported when upgrading between Lunaris builds (e.g., v3.12 to v3.13). Preserves installed apps and user data.
Install_Lunaris.bat — Windows Fastboot Installer
1 Download the latest Lunaris Fastboot ZIP from the Downloads page.
2 Extract the downloaded ZIP archive using 7-Zip or WinRAR into a folder on your PC.
3 Boot your Redmi K50i into Fastboot Mode (Vol Down + Power) and connect via USB.
4 Double-click Install_Lunaris.bat inside the extracted folder.
5 When prompted in the CMD window, type 1 for Clean Flash (or 2 for Dirty Flash).
6 Sit back! The automated script flashes all boot, vendor, and system partitions, then reboots your phone automatically.
// Verify fastboot connection (Optional CMD check)
C:\> fastboot devices
linux_installation.sh — Linux Fastboot Installer
1 Extract the Lunaris ZIP package to your desired directory.
2 Boot Redmi K50i into Fastboot Mode and connect to your Linux PC.
3 Open a Terminal in the extracted directory and grant execution permissions:
// Make installer script executable
$ chmod +x linux_installation.sh
// Execute script with sudo
$ sudo ./linux_installation.sh
4 Follow the interactive terminal prompts to select Clean or Dirty Flash. The device will reboot once complete.
install_termux.sh — Mobile-to-Mobile via OTG Cable
1 Install Termux on your secondary (host) Android phone.
2 Open Termux and install the required Android platform tools:
~ $ pkg update && pkg install android-tools
~ $ termux-setup-storage
3 Extract the Lunaris ZIP to your host phone's internal storage (e.g. Download/Lunaris).
4 Connect your K50i in Fastboot Mode to the host phone using an OTG adapter cable.
5 Navigate to the extracted folder in Termux and execute:
~ $ cd /sdcard/Download/Lunaris && chmod +x install_termux.sh && ./install_termux.sh
6 If Termux prompts with a USB Permission dialog popup on screen, tap OK / Allow.
Recovery Sideload — Boot, Vendor_Boot & ADB Sideload
1 Ensure your bootloader is unlocked, backup all your data, and install Xiaomi ADB/Fastboot drivers on your PC.
2 Download the ROM zip and Recovery files (boot.img and vendor_boot.img) from the Downloads page. Place them in your platform-tools folder.
3 Turn off your Redmi K50i, enter Fastboot Mode by holding Volume Down + Power, connect USB, and flash the boot and vendor_boot partitions:
// Flash Boot Image
> fastboot flash boot boot.img
// Flash Vendor Boot Image
> fastboot flash vendor_boot vendor_boot.img
4 To boot into recovery manually: Press and hold the Volume Up button and the Power button at the same time. Release the Power button as soon as the Redmi/Mi logo appears on the screen, while continuing to hold the Volume Up button until the recovery menu opens. Alternatively, run:
// Reboot into Recovery
> fastboot reboot recovery
5 Once in Custom Recovery, tap on Format Data / Factory Reset and confirm to wipe userdata.
6 Enable ADB Sideload: Go to Apply update → select Apply from ADB (ADB Sideload).
7 On your computer command prompt / terminal, run the sideload command with your ROM zip file:
// Sideload Lunaris ROM Zip
> adb sideload Lunaris-AOSP-xaga-UNOFFICIAL-3.13-GMS-2026110116.zip
8 Wait for sideloading to finish (100% or step 2/2). Then in Recovery tap Reboot system now. Enjoy Lunaris AOSP!

Frequently Asked Questions & Recovery

Q: How long does the first boot take?
The initial Android 16 ART runtime compilation and encryption take approximately 2 to 4 minutes. Do not force power-off during the animated Lunaris logo.
Q: What if Fastboot devices shows no output on Windows?
You need to install the Google USB Fastboot Driver via Windows Device Manager. Under "Other Devices" → "Android", right-click and update driver pointing to the extracted Google USB driver files.
Q: Do Banking Apps and Play Integrity work?
Yes! Lunaris AOSP includes built-in Play Integrity spoofing passing deviceIntegrity out of the box without requiring root. Banking apps, Google Pay, and streaming apps run seamlessly.
